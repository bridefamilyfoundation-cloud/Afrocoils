How it works

Built for the moment your hair won’t cooperate.

Twenty minutes, somewhere to be. No home screen to get past, no menu to read first.

  1. 01 · Hair SOS

    It opens on the rescue.

    Every launch lands on Hair SOS: the title, one button, and the way in right beneath it. People reach for this app mid-problem far more often than they browse it, so it doesn’t make you browse.

  2. 02 · What’s your emergency?

    Two doors. That’s the whole triage.

    Quick Hairstyle when you have somewhere to be. Hair Care when you want to know what your hair is and what it needs. A short list is what makes it triage rather than a menu.

  3. 03 · Quick Hairstyle

    Say how long you’ve got.

    %bands.labels% minutes. The library narrows to what fits the clock, then ranks against your hair. Every style was contributed by someone who wears it, with their own step-by-step method.

  4. 04 · Hair Care

    Know your hair, then look after it.

    A five-question porosity test, your density, curl pattern and texture. Then a care calendar that works out what’s due next, a product shelf filtered to your profile and your water — and when something’s wrong, the Quick Hair Check starts from the symptom.

  5. 05 · Hair Growth

    Measure what you keep.

    Hair grows steadily; the difference is how much survives. Log a length check and see retention against the half-inch-a-month average, plus your next milestone from neck to waist — dated from your own rate once you have sixty days of checks.

  6. 06 · My Hair Profile

    One quiz, and everything sharpens.

    The quiz asks about what you can see on your own head, not categories you’re meant to know already. The first answer — loose hair or locs — changes half the app.

Privacy

Your hair stays on your phone.

No account, no sign-in, no server holding your history. Your profile, length checks, calendar, photos and saved styles live in one document on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No crash or telemetry reporting
  • No model calls, ever

The app does ask this site whether a newer version is out. That request carries nothing about you, and you can switch it off in the app.

The one thing that ever leaves

  1. You choose to share a style

    Its write-up, its photos, the credit name you pick, and your word that the photos are yours to give.

  2. Saved on your phone first

    Marked Queued. If the send fails it waits and goes later, so a bad connection never costs you the write-up.

  3. Sent when you tap Share

    Nothing else from your profile goes with it — not your name, avatar, length history or calendar.

  4. A person reads it

    Nothing is published automatically. Withdraw it any time, and its photos are deleted from your phone too.

FAQ

Worth knowing first.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone, in one document, and nowhere else. Your hair profile, length history, calendar, photos and saved styles never leave the device — there’s no account, no sign-in and no server holding any of it. Uninstalling the app deletes it. The single exception is a style you deliberately contribute.

Is any of this AI-generated?

The words aren’t. Every guide, style and step was written by a person and ships inside the app. The style pictures are illustrations generated before release to show each written style front, back and side. There’s no chatbot, no generated advice and no model call behind any screen — which is also why the rescue flow works with the phone in aeroplane mode.

Is downloading an APK safe?

Download it only from this page. Android asks before it installs anything from outside an app store, and you can check the file against the SHA-256 checksum above. The app asks for two permissions, each only when you use the feature: the camera or photos when you add a picture, and notifications if you turn on a care reminder.

Will it update itself?

It tells you. When a new version is posted here, the app says so the next time you open it, and one tap downloads it. It installs over the old one, so your profile and history stay put — your phone still asks you to confirm. When AfroCoils reaches Google Play, the Play version will need this one uninstalled first, and because everything lives on your phone, uninstalling clears your profile and history.

Why are there no loc styles in the library?

Because nobody has contributed one yet. Every entry was written up by the person who wore it, and the app won’t fill the gap with loose-hair technique — on locs most of it does damage. Loc wearers do get Locs World and the care guides written separately for them.

Is it a substitute for a professional?

No. Everything in it is general guidance about hair; it can’t see your hair and doesn’t know your medical history. Sudden shedding, bald patches, persistent scalp pain or anything getting worse is a question for a dermatologist or a trichologist.
