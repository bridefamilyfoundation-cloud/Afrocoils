Download AfroCoils for Android

The hair app for Type 4 natural hair — 4A, 4B and 4C, loose or loc’d. Quick hairstyles for the time you have, hair care guides, growth tracking and a hair SOS that works with no signal.

Before you install

  • Free to download; every feature is for members. Try it free for three days, no card, then choose Monthly, 6 Months or Annual.
  • Updates install in place: the app tells you when there is one, and nothing installs without your tap.
  • Your hair profile stays on your phone. Once you are signed in, the app works offline.
  • Google Play — the listing is on its way. iPhone — not yet.
